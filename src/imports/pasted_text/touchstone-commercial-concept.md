Design a premium, modern website concept for "TouchStone Commercial", a US-based commercial outdoor lighting company serving Minnesota and surrounding regions.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS CONTEXT:

TouchStone Commercial specializes exclusively in COMMERCIAL outdoor lighting for this website.

Do NOT position the company as a residential lighting company.
Do NOT make this look like an e-commerce or online lighting store.

The company wants to attract larger commercial projects from:
- Property owners
- Commercial developers
- Architects
- Landscape architects
- General contractors
- Property managers
- Facility managers
- Hospitality businesses
- Multifamily developments
- Retail properties
- Office and corporate properties
- Medical facilities
- Other commercial properties

The website's primary objective is to generate qualified commercial project inquiries while establishing TouchStone as an experienced, technically capable commercial lighting expert.

CORE DIFFERENTIATORS:

1. COMMERCIAL EXPERTISE
TouchStone has significant experience executing commercial outdoor lighting projects.

2. CUSTOM MANUFACTURING
One of TouchStone's biggest competitive advantages is that they manufacture their own lighting fixtures and can customize/build lighting solutions around the requirements of individual projects.

The messaging should communicate:
"Your project doesn't have to fit our fixtures. We can build the right lighting solution around your project."

3. PROPRIETARY iPAD LIGHTING PLANNING APPLICATION
TouchStone uses a custom-built iPad application during commercial projects to create lighting plans and visualize fixture placement.

The application gives clients greater clarity about the proposed lighting solution before installation.

The website should showcase this technology as a genuine business advantage, NOT as a gimmicky technology feature.

The story should be:

UNDERSTAND → DESIGN → VISUALIZE → CUSTOM BUILD → INSTALL → FINAL RESULT

4. PROJECT-BASED PROOF
The website should strongly showcase completed commercial projects through high-quality case studies.

Each case study should demonstrate:
- The property
- The challenge
- Lighting strategy
- iPad lighting plan
- Products used
- Custom manufacturing where applicable
- Installation
- Final nighttime result

DESIGN DIRECTION:

Create a sophisticated, premium, architectural and technically credible visual identity.

The website should feel:
- Premium
- Established
- Architectural
- Professional
- Technical
- Innovative
- Trustworthy
- Commercial
- High-ticket

Avoid:
- Generic landscaping-company aesthetics
- Cheap contractor website aesthetics
- E-commerce/store aesthetics
- Excessive gradients
- Excessive animations
- Generic stock-photo feeling
- Too many cards
- Cluttered navigation
- Bright consumer-oriented colors

Use large cinematic commercial outdoor-lighting photography, especially nighttime architectural and landscape lighting.

The lighting itself should create the visual drama.

Use a dark, sophisticated visual direction with warm lighting imagery, restrained neutral typography, generous whitespace, strong editorial layouts and subtle premium details.

Do not over-design the interface.

TYPOGRAPHY:

Use a modern premium sans-serif typeface such as Inter, Manrope, Geist, Helvetica Neue, or a similar high-quality sans-serif.

Use large editorial headlines.

Strong contrast between large headlines and concise supporting copy.

LAYOUT:

Desktop-first design at approximately 1440px width.

Also ensure the layouts are responsive and can translate cleanly to tablet and mobile.

Use a 12-column grid.

Large imagery.

Generous spacing.

Strong visual hierarchy.

Navigation should be minimal.

CONTENT & IMAGERY GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------

Prioritize real TouchStone photography whenever available.

Use placeholder image blocks where real client assets are not yet available.

Do not fabricate real client names, project statistics, certifications, awards, customer logos or technical claims.

Clearly use placeholder labels for content that must be replaced after client discovery.

Use actual TouchStone branding/logo if available from the existing website.

The design should make the existing website feel outdated by comparison, without explicitly criticizing it.

The visual concept should communicate:

"TouchStone is not simply a company selling outdoor lighting products.

TouchStone is a commercial lighting partner that understands the property, designs the solution, visualizes it, manufactures what is required, and executes the installation."

Overall goal:

Create a Figma concept strong enough to present to the business owner as a proposed transformation of their digital presence.

The final result should feel like the website of a sophisticated commercial lighting design and manufacturing company capable of handling high-value commercial projects.
